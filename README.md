# ![Unown](/assets/sprites/pokemon-icons/1060.png) Tourney Tracker

Always wanted to note your Pokémon TCG tournament results, but in an actually good and convenient way? Look no further. With Tourney Tracker, you can turn tournaments into memories that last forever. Track your wins and losses, add photos, do some things with sorting and stuff.

Tourney Tracker is an Android app that stores all your data on *your* device. No account, no internet connection needed, no money that you need to pay, no relying on another service to keep your tournament results alive. I could die in 50 years and you would still have all your tournament results with you until the heat death of the universe or until all phones are obsolete. But even when phones are obsolete the app will work.

## ![Porygon2](/assets/sprites/pokemon-icons/233.png) How to get it

On this GitHub page, there should be a section that says **Releases**, and in there you'll find a `.apk` file. Simply download this file to your phone and open it to install it. 

Your phone will give you one or two warnings about installing apps from untrusted sources and that's because I'm not putting this on the Google Play store because I assume that costs money (not sure actually?), and because I do some minor copyright infringement. Anyway, make sure to click like "Allow apps from untrusted sources" and "Install anyway". 

I'm not hacking your phone or stealing your data, this app is fully open source so you can even double check. I wouldn't even know how to do it.

## ![Rotom](/assets/sprites/pokemon-icons/479.png) Features

### Add Events

- Keep track of the decks you played and the opponents you faced
- Add notes and photos to turn boring data into memories

### Timeline

- See an overview of your events in a nice chronological timeline
- Use dates and event types to sort and filter easily
- Add markers to store things that aren't tournaments -- when rotation happened, when you started playing a new deck, etc. -- you can even ignore all the Pokémon stuff and use this app as a journal

### Themes

- Comes in **four** different colour schemes: default, Sylveon, Knospi and Umbreon.
